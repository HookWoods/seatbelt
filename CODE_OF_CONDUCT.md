# Code of Conduct

Seatbelt exists for people who are new to building software. Many of them are told their work is "just vibe code". Here, nobody is made to feel stupid for not knowing something.

## What we expect

- Be patient and kind, especially with beginners. Explain in plain words; never mock a question.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Give credit, and accept feedback without taking it personally.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the people Seatbelt serves.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination based on who someone is: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing secrets, keys or personal data found in someone's project or report. If you see one, tell them privately so they can replace it.
- Pull requests or issues produced in bulk by an agent with no human review.

## Where this applies

In the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through GitHub, using the private contact options on the maintainers' profiles, or with GitHub's built-in "Report content" button on any comment. Reports are read by the maintainers only and handled confidentially.

## What happens next

Depending on the situation, maintainers may ask for a correction, hide or edit content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
